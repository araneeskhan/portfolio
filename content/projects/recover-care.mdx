RecoverCare covers the period between surgical discharge and full recovery, when patients are managing their own care with the least supervision. Patients log a guided daily check-in — mood, pain, symptoms, and vitals — track medication doses, and message their care team directly. An alert engine grades every submission across four severity tiers and escalates risky symptom combinations rather than isolated readings. Incoming messages are scored for urgency so time-critical ones surface first, and logged vitals feed a local regression that projects a recovery trend. Built as a React Native client over an Express and PostgreSQL API with Prisma.
