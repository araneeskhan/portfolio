An e-commerce platform built as two distinct surfaces over one catalogue. Shoppers browse and filter products, manage a cart, see recommendations, and move through checkout; administrators manage products and inventory from a separate dashboard with its own permissions. Built with Next.js and a Node backend, styled with Chakra UI and Tailwind, and laid out to work from phone to desktop.
