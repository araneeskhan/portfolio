An end-to-end sports management system for a university athletics department, built as a React Native app for students and a React web console for administrators. It replaces manual ground booking and equipment issuance with a scheduled, auditable process: a Round Robin allocator assigns ground slots against real venue constraints, live weather data shifts the recommended times, and a TensorFlow.js classifier checks equipment photos at the point of upload. Student questions are answered by a Flask service built on Sentence Transformers over a 4,000-pair question set compiled from departmental records and scraped HEC sports data.
