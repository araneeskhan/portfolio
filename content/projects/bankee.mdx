Bankee is a mobile banking client built with React Native, Expo SDK 54, and Firebase. It covers the everyday surface of a retail bank: biometric sign-in with encrypted local credentials, virtual cards that can be frozen and unfrozen on the spot, peer-to-peer transfers by account number, IBAN, or QR code, group bill splitting with per-person amounts and request tracking, categorized spending analytics across weekly, monthly, and yearly windows, goal-based savings vaults, exportable receipts, and subscription and utility bill payment. Every balance-changing operation runs inside a Firestore transaction, so a transfer either completes on both sides or not at all.
