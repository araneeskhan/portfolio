QuickBite is a food ordering app built around a deliberate constraint: every ranking and recommendation runs on the device. Menu search uses TF-IDF scoring weighted across titles, descriptions, and ingredients; recommendations compare ingredient sets by Jaccard similarity against a user's favorites, with a popularity fallback for new accounts; delivery estimates come from a linear model over cart size, category mix, and base preparation times; and cross-sell suggestions look for gaps in the basket rather than pushing the highest-margin item. The result is instant response with no per-query cost and no order data sent to a ranking service. Ordering covers meal customization, multiple payment methods, live order tracking, reviews, and re-ordering from history.
